Off-screen

Four drills a page can't run for you

5 minutes · daily

The blank skeleton

Open an empty workbook and type the row labels — all three statements plus the four schedules — from memory. No numbers, no formulas, no filing open. Stop when you can do it without a gap. This is what makes the first fifteen minutes of a real build automatic.

10 minutes · daily

Sabotage

Take a finished model, break exactly one link — flip a sign, delete a cash flow line, hardcode an opening balance — save it, and walk away. Come back and time yourself finding it. This is the single highest-yield exercise in the whole program, because debugging is the skill that decays first.

3 minutes · daily

Out loud

Say a full three-statement ripple aloud, end to end, including the balance check at the finish. Speaking it uses different retrieval than typing it, and it is exactly the form the question takes when somebody asks you across a desk.

1 hour · daily

Mouse out of reach

Physically move it. Navigate, select, and format with the keyboard only. The goal isn't raw speed — it's that moving around the grid stops consuming attention you need for the accounting.

How to sequence them: warm up with two on-screen drills (about five minutes), then the blank skeleton, then your build for the day. Save sabotage for after the build, while a finished model is in front of you. The eight-week program has the full schedule.
