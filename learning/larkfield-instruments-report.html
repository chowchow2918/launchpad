Notes to the Financial Statements

$ millions unless stated
1

Revenue

Revenue grew 8.4% in FY2023 and 6.8% in FY2024. Management expects mid-single-digit growth to continue over the medium term as instrument replacement cycles normalise. No single customer accounts for more than 5% of revenue.

2

Selling, general and administrative

SG&A is substantially fixed. The group operates two manufacturing sites and a single distribution centre, and headcount in these functions has been broadly flat across the periods presented. Increases reflect wage and facilities inflation rather than volume.

3

Working capital

Receivables days have lengthened as the distributor channel has grown, and management has set a target of returning to below 52 days. Inventory has been built ahead of a product transition scheduled for FY2025.

4

Property, plant and equipment

Capital expenditure has run at 5.4% to 5.6% of revenue. Depreciation is charged on a straight-line basis and has approximated 12.7% to 13.5% of the opening net book value in each period.

5

Borrowings

The term loan amortises at $20.0m per year and matures in FY2034. Short-term borrowings are uncommitted working-capital lines renewed annually. The group also holds an undrawn $150.0m revolving credit facility maturing FY2028, which requires a minimum cash balance of $60.0m to be maintained.

6

Income taxes

The effective tax rate was 24.5% in FY2022 and FY2023 and 24.0% in FY2024, reflecting a change in the geographic mix of profits. The group holds no material loss carryforwards. Income taxes payable represented 60, 62 and 61 days of the tax charge in FY2022, FY2023 and FY2024 respectively.

7

Equity and distributions

Dividends declared represented 39.8%, 35.7% and 33.0% of net income across the three years, and the board has indicated a target payout of approximately one third of earnings. In FY2024 the group repurchased $8.0m of common equity under a programme authorised in November 2023. Dividends declared equalled dividends paid in each period.

8

Goodwill and intangible assets

Goodwill and intangible assets arose on the acquisition of a calibration services business in FY2019. Both are considered to have indefinite useful lives, are not amortised, and are tested annually for impairment. No impairment has been recognised in the periods presented.
